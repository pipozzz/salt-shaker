  <Card title="Fleet at a glance" icon="laptop">
    Status, OS, IPs, CPU/RAM and roles of every minion. Grains and compiled pillar per minion.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Compliance" icon="approve-check">
    Dry-run the highstate across a target, see what drifted and remediate with one click.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Run & orchestrate" icon="rocket">
    Any execution module against any target, with live results. Orchestrate files with dry-run.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Users, roles, audit" icon="seti:lock">
    Local accounts or single sign-on, with roles from your IdP's groups. Every change is audited.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Patches & vulnerabilities" icon="warning">
    Pending upgrades across the fleet, and known CVEs in installed packages from OSV.dev. Upgrade just the security fixes, or accept a risk with a reason.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Query with osquery" icon="magnifier">
    Ask every minion a question in SQL (ports, users, SSH keys, processes) and get one table back.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Schedules & alerts" icon="setting">
    Nightly compliance checks, patch and vulnerability scans and watched osquery queries, with alerts to Slack, Microsoft Teams or any webhook.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
